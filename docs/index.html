About this policy

This policy covers the prayer-times app Salah time and its home-screen widget. This version does not require an account and does not include Google sign-in, prayer tracking, Hifz tracking, advertising, analytics or an app crash-reporting service.

Location and permissions

Salah time uses a location to calculate prayer times. You can choose a city from the bundled offline city list without granting location permission. City searches are performed on your device.

If you choose “Use device location”, the app asks Android for approximate or precise location permission. It uses the location Android provides and your device time zone. While device-location mode is selected, the app may request an updated location when it returns to the foreground. It does not request background location permission or continuously track your movements.

Android location services may use GPS, Wi-Fi or mobile networks according to your device settings. Those system services operate under their providers’ privacy policies; they are separate from Salah time.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the latest selected location’s name, latitude, longitude, time zone and whether it came from device location or a manual city selection. It stores these in the app’s private storage so prayer times remain available offline. It does not keep a location history.

The widget reads this saved location to calculate prayer times without requesting a new device location in the background. A selected city name and prayer times may be visible to anyone who can see your home screen.

The optional “Timely widget updates” setting opens Android’s “Alarms & reminders” access screen. Allowing this access lets the app schedule more precise widget refreshes at prayer-time changes, including while the device sleeps. These refreshes run on your device without playing a sound or turning on the screen. You can revoke this access in Android Settings; the widget continues to work, but updates may be delayed. Android power restrictions can also delay updates when access is allowed.

Sharing and network use

Prayer-time calculations run on your device. The app does not upload your saved location or city searches to Inma, run a sync service, or sell or share this information with advertisers. This version does not request Android’s Internet permission. The bundled city data and calculation library work offline.

Your choices and deletion

You can change to a manually selected city in Settings → Location. Saving a new location replaces the previous saved location. You can revoke location permission in Android Settings; revoking permission does not delete the location already saved in the app.

The saved location is retained until you replace it or remove the app’s stored data. To delete it, use Android Settings → Apps → Salah time → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or uninstall the app and remove its data. Menu names can vary by device. Clearing storage resets the app’s location setup.

Support messages

If you email shaolinzaman@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and keep it only as long as needed to handle the request. You can ask us to delete support correspondence using the same address. Email is handled by email providers under their own privacy policies; the app does not send email or attach location information automatically.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions, contact Inma at shaolinzaman@gmail.com.
